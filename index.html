The Gap

Every one of your cars has a photo.
Almost none of them have a video.

The modern car buyer scrolls Reels and Shorts the way they used to walk your lot — slowly, comparing, getting a feel. That walk now happens on a feed that racks up 44 billion views, and your dealership isn't on it.

Not because you don't want to be. Because posting video every workday means someone has to choose the car, write what to say, shoot it, edit it, and remember to post it — and your best salesperson is already selling.

The studios that promise to fix this demand skills and setup your lot won't do. The editors are great — for a human with 60–90 spare minutes a video.

Winning dealers post every workday. Most independents can't. That gap is the product.

№ 03

What's under the hood

01 Trend-driven car selection

The machine watches what your community finishes, rewatches, and shares — and points the camera at the car most likely to earn it. A slow mover with a hot search trend beats a fast mover with nothing to say.

02 VIN-aware scripts

Trim, mileage, options, price — read from the vehicle itself and worked into a script that sounds like your best closer, not a template. The hook line up top? That's the machine's handwriting.

03 One-to-many publishing

Film once. Out come the 9:16 reel, the Shorts cut, the TikTok version, and the landscape embed for your VDP. Sized, captioned, and queued across platforms in one motion.

04 The analytics loop

Watch-time and leads close the loop. What buyers watched tonight changes what gets filmed tomorrow. A feed that learns beats a feed that posts.

05 No creators required

No videographer, no editor, no "social person." The decision-making is the software's; the twenty seconds is anyone's. Your front desk can run it on a Tuesday.

06 Auto-translation FUTURE

Queued next: one script, many markets — Spanish-first cuts for the lots that need them. On the roadmap, not yet in the pilot.

Questions from the desk

Do I need a videographer?

No. That's the point. Any staffer with a phone can film the 20 seconds — walking the car, reading the hook the machine wrote. There's no creator to hire and no editing skill to find.

Whose footage is it?

Yours. Every frame comes from your lot and your phones; every finished video comes out of your pipeline. Pilot or not, you keep everything we make together.

What about my platforms?

Instagram, YouTube and TikTok connect once, through your API keys — your accounts, your login. Posts go out on your schedule, auto-posted or queued for your review, plus a landscape cut sized for your VDPs.

How fast, really?

About 20 minutes from raw phone footage to publish-ready video, most days. The deciding — which car, what hook, which format — happens before you've had coffee.

What does it cost?

The two-week pilot is free — no card, no contract. After the pilot we'd discuss pricing with you directly; we're not printing a number here that we haven't agreed with a dealer yet. The ROI column above prices the thing you're already paying for instead.

Is this live everywhere today?

It's in pilot — that's who you'd be first. Forecourt runs on a dealer's real inventory and real accounts during the two weeks, and the pilot dealers' results are what we're building the next version from.
