The gap

Your inventory speaks in stills.
Your buyers watch in video.

Forty cars on the lot, forty photo-only listings, one silent feed. Meanwhile the kid two lots over posts a walkaround every workday — and the leads go to him. The winning pattern is boring and it works: show up on the feed, every workday.

Most independents can’t. Nobody on staff has the time, the eye, or the stomach for “be a content team.” That gap is exactly what we built Forecourt for.

  • The decision tax — choose the car, write the hook, cut the clip, remember to post. Every. Single. Day.
  • The creator tax — $10–16/hr for whoever’s free, or $500–$3,000/mo for an agency.
  • The time tax — 60–90 minutes in CapCut per video. That’s another job nobody was hired for.

How it works

Five steps. One of them is yours.

The machine carries the other four.

1

Drop in your inventory

Every car, every stat — pulled straight from your DMS or a spreadsheet. One upload, auto-refresh.

2

Connect your socials

Instagram, YouTube, TikTok. Once, with API keys. After that, posting is something that just happens.

3

The system decides what to film

Which car, what to say, which format — tuned to what your community actually watches and what’s trending in your market. No guesswork from your team, because there is no guessing.

4

Your staff films 20 seconds

Raw phone footage. Walk-around, wheels, interior sweep. That’s the entire human job. No creator, no set, no script memorized.

5

~20 minutes later: published

A finished video comes out — vertical-first for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, plus a VDP-ready landscape cut — and posts itself or queues on your schedule. Then analytics feed the loop: what your buyers watched changes tomorrow’s pick.

Straight answers

Dealer questions,
answered plainly.

Do I need a videographer?

No. That’s the whole point. Any staff member who can walk a car with a phone for twenty seconds is your “content team.” Forecourt handles the choosing, writing, editing, and posting.

Whose footage is it?

Yours. Your lot, your cars, your phone, your accounts. During the pilot we’re building your pipeline, not a stock library.

What about my platforms — Instagram, YouTube, TikTok?

You connect them once with API keys. After that the system posts the right format to the right place, or queues it for a time you pick. No logging in, no uploading by hand.

How fast, honestly?

About twenty minutes from raw clip landing to a publish-ready cut — that’s the number on the countdown above, and it’s the cap on human effort too: twenty seconds of it is yours.

What does it cost?

The pilot costs $0 for two weeks, and we’re not going to pretend there’s a public price list before we’ve run five real lots through it. You’ll get a fair, per-lot quote when the pilot ends — the ROI calculator above shows what not doing this costs instead.

I’m not technical. Is that a problem?

Setup is: a spreadsheet or DMS export of inventory, and the social keys. We sit on the call and do it with you. After week one, the only daily job is handing someone a phone.
